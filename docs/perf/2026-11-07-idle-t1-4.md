- **Commit:** the commit that adds this file (measured from its working tree, on top of dd86196)
- **Hardware:** `dev-m5` (MacBook Pro Mac17,2, Apple M5, 10 cores (4 performance, 6 efficiency), 32 GB, macOS 27.2
