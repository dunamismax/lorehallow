The idea

A world that's already there

You log in, and other people are already around. Someone jogs past on the road. A group is fighting something at the edge of the woods. The town square is busy, and the lanterns are lit whether or not you're looking.

Lorehallow is a small online fantasy world in the spirit of early World of Warcraft: a persistent place with zones, quests and grouping, drawn in warm, readable pixel art. It stays small on purpose. One handcrafted town that feels alive is worth more than ten empty continents.

More of the world

{{template "sectionhead" (dict "Icon" "source" "Title" "Built in the open" "ID" "open" "Kicker" "Source and plan")}}

All of Lorehallow is public: the Go server, the browser client, the pixel art, the plan and every decision behind it. The server is the part this project is proudest of. It's built from the ground up to be fast, reliable and secure, and the goal is hundreds to thousands of players at once. That's a target, not a claim: no player count goes on this site until it has been measured.

Every phase ends with a written walkthrough that explains the networking or backend idea behind it, so the project doubles as a course in building an online game.
