When can I play?

Phase 2 of the plan ends with the first playable version: two players in one small world, moving around and seeing each other. After that, every phase ships a new version, and it goes live here and at play.lorehallow.com the same day. Pre-1.0 versions are numbered after their phase, so Phase 2 is v0.2.0.

{{end}}

What you'll need

  • A current desktop browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a desktop or laptop computer.
  • A keyboard, and an internet connection: the world lives on the server.

It's free. There are no downloads, installers or launchers: every new version is simply there the next time you load the page.
